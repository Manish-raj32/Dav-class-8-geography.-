Natural Resources: Land, Soil and Water

Land Resources

  • Land covers 29% of Earth.
  • Used for farming, houses, roads, industries and mining.
  • Land use depends on physical and human factors.

Soil Resources

  • Soil is formed by weathering.
  • Contains humus, minerals, air and water.
  • Very important for agriculture.

Factors of Soil Formation

  1. Parent Rock
  2. Topography
  3. Climate
  4. Vegetation
  5. Time

Soil Conservation

  • Afforestation
  • Contour Ploughing
  • Terrace Farming
  • Crop Rotation
  • Shelter Belts

Water Resources

  • 97% Saline Water
  • 3% Fresh Water
  • Main source of fresh water is rainfall.

Water Pollution

  • Industrial Waste
  • Sewage
  • Diseases: Cholera, Typhoid, Jaundice

Water Conservation

  • Rainwater Harvesting
  • Water Recycling
  • Drip Irrigation
  • Sprinkler Irrigation
